# 00.2 — Visual Design System

**Status:** Starter summary; the owner's full visual-system document takes precedence after upload and reconciliation.

## Candy Glass identity
Material-led, pearlescent, glossy, translucent, tactile, dimensional, expressive and polished. It must feel art-directed, not like a generic pastel productivity template. Transparency is one material property, not the entire design system.

## Palette
Pearlescent pink, spring/apple green, white/pearl, lavender, aqua and mint, with supporting tones only where hierarchy requires them. Maintain readable contrast. Avoid indiscriminate rainbow gradients and muddy combinations.

## Material vocabulary
- **Pearl Glass:** luminous translucent panels and controlled highlights.
- **Apple Jelly:** rich translucent green accents and selected surfaces.
- **Candy Plastic:** tactile, solid controls with dimensional edges.
- **Opal Finish:** restrained iridescent shifts for special surfaces.
- **Frosted Acrylic:** quiet secondary panels and overlays.
- **Liquid Pearl:** small reflective accents.

Give each component a reason for its material, edge, lighting, depth and state treatment. Do not put every component in the same glass capsule.

## Composition and typography
Use intentional layering, consistent light direction, controlled bevels, purposeful shadows and reflective highlights. Avoid random blobs, excessive rounded rectangles, arbitrary stickers, blur-only glassmorphism, generic SaaS layouts and clutter. Preserve the smooth polished typography the owner liked; confirm exact font family, weights, scale and licensing from uploaded docs or source. Do not casually substitute it.

## Pixel-art boundary
Pixel art belongs to world scenery, characters, companions, props and collectibles. Candy Glass governs the mobile shell, navigation, productivity interfaces, forms, controls and system panels. Each layer retains its own visual logic.

## Motion and states
Motion should communicate state or atmosphere, not delay work. Define default, pressed, focused, selected, disabled, loading, success and error states. Essential information must not depend on hover. Respect reduced motion and device performance.

## Quality rejection list
No generic pastel SaaS, blur-only glassmorphism, random gradients, inconsistent lighting, universal pixel filter, placeholder icons where custom artwork is required, decorative nonfunctional controls or clutter that harms usability.

For every major component specify purpose, hierarchy, responsive layout, material, color, typography, border, lighting, shadow, art, states, motion, accessibility, empty/error states and acceptance criteria.
